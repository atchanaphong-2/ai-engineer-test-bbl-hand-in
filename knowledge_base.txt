Company Overview: Northwind Traders is a mid-sized logistics and import/export company founded in 2004, headquartered in Singapore with regional offices in Bangkok, Ho Chi Minh City, and Jakarta. The company employs approximately 850 people across finance, operations, warehousing, and client services. Northwind's mission is to make cross-border trade simple and transparent for small and medium-sized businesses across Southeast Asia. The company is privately held, with its founding family retaining a majority stake, and reported annual revenue of approximately 210 million USD in its most recent fiscal year.

Company Leadership and Milestones: Northwind Traders is led by CEO Mei-Ling Tan, who co-founded the company in 2004 and previously worked in freight forwarding at a global logistics carrier. The company reached its first major milestone in 2011 when it opened its Bangkok regional office, followed by Ho Chi Minh City in 2015 and Jakarta in 2019. Northwind launched its Freight Tracking Platform in 2017 and its Customs Documentation Assistant add-on in 2021. The company was named "Logistics Innovator of the Year" by the Southeast Asia Trade Council in 2022.

International Travel Policy
Document ID: HR-POL-014
Effective Date: 2024-01-15
Version: 3.2
Owner: Global Mobility & Travel Team
Employees traveling internationally for business purposes must submit a travel request through the company portal (TravelHub) at least 10 business days before departure. All international trips require pre-approval from the employee's direct manager and the regional finance controller, and trips lasting longer than 14 consecutive days additionally require sign-off from a Regional Director. Economy class is standard for flights under 6 hours; premium economy is permitted for flights exceeding 6 hours, and business class is authorized only for flights over 10 hours when the traveling employee holds a Director-level title or above. Employees must use the company's approved travel agency, Journey Corporate Travel, for all bookings to remain covered under the corporate travel insurance policy underwritten by Pacific Shield Insurance (policy number PSI-88214).

International Travel Policy — Exceptions and Per Diem: Personal travel combined with business trips is allowed but must be clearly disclosed on the TravelHub request at least 5 business days before departure; the company will only reimburse the cost of the business-related portion of the trip, calculated using the "shortest business itinerary" comparison method. Visa and passport renewal fees required specifically for a business trip are reimbursable with a receipt, but routine passport renewal is not covered. Per diem for international trips is set at 75 USD per day in Tier 1 cities (Singapore, Tokyo, Hong Kong) and 55 USD per day elsewhere, and does not require itemized meal receipts under these caps. Employees who miss the 10-business-day booking window due to a client-driven schedule change may request an expedited exception from their manager, though airfare booked within 72 hours of departure requires additional Regional Director approval regardless of employee level.

Domestic Travel Policy: Domestic travel within the country of an employee's home office does not require advance approval if the total cost is under 300 USD and the trip is completed within a single day. Multi-day domestic trips still require manager approval but can be submitted with only 3 business days' notice. Mileage reimbursement for personal vehicle use is set at the standard national rate, updated annually each January, and is submitted through the same TravelHub portal used for international travel. Rideshare and taxi fares under 50 USD per trip do not require a receipt, but any single fare above that threshold must be itemized. Employees are encouraged to book domestic flights at least 7 days in advance where possible, since Journey Corporate Travel's fare-lock program can reduce costs by roughly 15 to 20 percent compared to last-minute bookings.

Expense Reimbursement Policy: All business expenses must be submitted with itemized receipts within 30 days of being incurred, through the NorthwindExpense mobile app. Expenses submitted after 30 days will not be reimbursed except in cases of documented extenuating circumstances approved by the finance director. Meals during business travel are capped at 60 USD per day domestically and follow the international per diem rates listed in the travel policy for trips abroad; alcohol is not a reimbursable expense under any circumstance, including client entertainment. Individual expense line items under 25 USD do not require an attached receipt image, but must still be categorized correctly in the app. Expenses submitted in a foreign currency are converted to USD using the exchange rate in effect on the date of purchase, as recorded automatically by the NorthwindExpense app.

Remote Work Policy: Northwind Traders operates a hybrid work model. Employees are expected to work from an approved office location at least 3 days per week, with Tuesday and Thursday designated as mandatory in-office collaboration days. Fully remote arrangements may be approved on a case-by-case basis for employees who have been with the company for at least 12 months and have manager sign-off. Remote work from outside the employee's home country for more than 20 consecutive days requires HR and legal approval due to tax and visa implications. Employees approved for hybrid or remote work receive a one-time 400 USD home-office equipment stipend, covering items such as a monitor, chair, or ergonomic keyboard, claimable once every 3 years through the NorthwindExpense app. Home internet connections used for company work must meet a minimum of 25 Mbps download speed, and company laptops may not be connected to public Wi-Fi without an active VPN session.

Paid Leave Policy: Full-time employees accrue 18 days of paid annual leave per year, in addition to public holidays observed in their home office location. Unused leave may be carried over into the following year up to a maximum of 5 days; any leave beyond that cap is forfeited unless local labor law requires otherwise. Sick leave is provided separately at 10 days per year and does not require a doctor's note for absences of 2 days or fewer; absences of 3 or more consecutive days require a medical certificate submitted to HR within 5 business days of returning to work. Leave requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance during the peak shipping season (October through December) and may be denied during this window if more than 20 percent of a department's headcount has already been approved for leave on the same dates.

Parental Leave Policy
Document ID: HR-POL-021
Effective Date: 2023-06-01
Version: 2.0
Owner: People & Culture Team
Primary caregivers are entitled to 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave, and secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks of fully paid leave. Parental leave can be taken continuously or split into two blocks within the first 12 months after the child's birth or adoption, subject to manager notification at least 6 weeks in advance where possible. Adoptive and foster parents are eligible for the same leave entitlements as biological parents, provided the placement is expected to last at least 6 months. Employees returning from primary caregiver leave may request a phased return-to-work arrangement of up to 4 weeks at 60 percent working hours on full pay, subject to manager approval. Health insurance and other benefits continue uninterrupted throughout the full parental leave period.

Code of Conduct
Document ID: LEGAL-POL-002
Version: 4.1
Owner: Ethics & Compliance Team
All employees are expected to act with honesty, integrity, and respect toward colleagues, clients, and partners. Conflicts of interest, including outside employment or financial interests in competing businesses, must be disclosed to HR in writing within 10 business days of arising. Gifts or entertainment from clients, vendors, or partners with a value over 75 USD must be declared to a manager and logged in the Ethics Register; cash or cash-equivalent gifts of any value are prohibited outright. Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation of any kind are strictly prohibited and will result in disciplinary action up to and including termination. Employees who witness or experience a violation are encouraged to report it through the confidential ethics hotline (operated by an independent third party) without fear of retaliation; all reports are acknowledged within 2 business days and investigated within 30 days.

Data Security Policy
Document ID: SEC-POL-007
Classification: Internal
Owner: Information Security Team
Employees must use company-issued devices with full-disk encryption and up-to-date endpoint protection software for all work involving client shipment or customs data. Client data must never be stored on personal cloud drives, USB drives, or personal email accounts. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all systems that store shipment, customer, or financial data, and passwords must be at least 14 characters and rotated every 90 days. Any suspected data breach must be reported to the security team within 1 hour of discovery via the #security-incident channel or the 24-hour security hotline. Confirmed breaches affecting customer data trigger a tiered response: Tier 1 (fewer than 50 records) requires internal notification only, Tier 2 (50 to 5,000 records) requires customer notification within 72 hours, and Tier 3 (over 5,000 records) additionally requires regulatory notification per local data protection law. Decommissioned laptops and hard drives must be physically destroyed by an approved vendor, not simply wiped, before disposal.

Product Overview - Freight Tracking Platform: Northwind's core product is a freight tracking platform that gives clients real-time visibility into the location and customs status of their shipments. Launched in 2017, the platform integrates with over 40 regional carriers and customs authorities, consolidating tracking data into a single dashboard. Clients receive automated alerts for customs holds, delays, and delivery confirmations via email, SMS, or webhook integration with their own systems. The platform is offered in three tiers: Starter (up to 100 shipments per month, dashboard access only), Growth (up to 1,000 shipments per month, adds SMS and webhook alerts), and Enterprise (unlimited shipments, adds a dedicated API with a 99.9 percent uptime SLA and a named account manager). A public REST API is available on the Growth and Enterprise tiers, with rate limits of 60 and 600 requests per minute respectively.

Product Overview - Customs Documentation Assistant: The Customs Documentation Assistant is an add-on tool, launched in 2021, that helps exporters automatically generate commercial invoices, packing lists, and certificates of origin based on a shipment's product and destination country. It currently supports customs documentation formats for 18 countries across Southeast Asia and East Asia. It flags missing or inconsistent information before submission to reduce customs clearance delays, and maintains a searchable archive of past shipment documents for audit purposes, retained for 7 years to satisfy typical customs record-keeping requirements. The add-on is priced separately from the core Freight Tracking Platform at 15 USD per shipment document generated, with volume discounts available above 500 documents per month.

General Facts About Cats: Cats are small domesticated carnivorous mammals that have lived alongside humans for roughly 9,000 years, originally valued for their ability to control rodent populations near early grain stores. A cat's whiskers are highly sensitive tactile organs that help it judge whether it can fit through a narrow space and to sense nearby objects in low light. Domestic cats spend an average of 12 to 16 hours a day sleeping, with kittens and older cats sleeping even more. Unlike dogs, most cats are obligate carnivores, meaning their diet must be primarily meat-based to supply nutrients like taurine that they cannot synthesize on their own. The average domestic cat lives 13 to 17 years, though indoor-only cats tend to live longer than cats with regular outdoor access, largely due to reduced exposure to traffic, predators, and disease.

Cat Senses and Physical Traits: A cat's hearing range extends up to roughly 64,000 Hz, far beyond human hearing (around 20,000 Hz) and even beyond most dogs, allowing them to detect the high-pitched sounds made by small rodents. Cats are considered nearsighted compared to humans but have excellent low-light vision, thanks to a reflective layer behind the retina called the tapetum lucidum, which also causes the familiar "eye shine" in photos. A healthy adult cat has 30 permanent teeth, and most domestic cats can run at speeds up to 30 miles per hour in short bursts, though they are built for sprinting rather than sustained running.

Cat Behavior and Communication: Cats communicate through a combination of vocalizations, body language, and scent marking. Purring is most commonly associated with contentment, but cats have also been observed purring when injured or anxious, which some researchers believe may serve a self-soothing or healing function. A cat rubbing its head or body against a person or object is depositing scent from glands on its face, marking the person or object as familiar and safe. Tail position is one of the clearest indicators of mood: an upright tail generally signals confidence and friendliness, while a puffed-up tail signals fear or aggression. Adult cats rarely meow at each other; meowing is primarily a behavior cats direct at humans, believed to have developed because it reliably gets a person's attention.
